# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $t>0$. Among all nondegenerate tetrahedra whose four face areas are
$$
1,\quad 1,\quad t,\quad t,
$$
determine the largest possible volume.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is primarily Euclidean, Coordinate, and Transformational Geometry and Solid geometry: it asks for the sharp maximum volume of a tetrahedron under prescribed face-area data. The solution uses the geometry of oriented face-area vectors, their closure relation, and a reconstruction showing that the extremal vector configuration is realized by an actual tetrahedron.

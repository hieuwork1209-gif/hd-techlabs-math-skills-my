# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $\mathcal V$ be the category of finite-dimensional vector spaces over $\mathbb F_2$ and linear maps. Define the endofunctor $T:\mathcal V\to\mathcal V$ by
$$
T(V)=V^{\otimes3},
\qquad
T(f)=f^{\otimes3}.
$$
A natural endomorphism $E:T\Rightarrow T$ is called idempotent if
$$
E_V^2=E_V
$$
for every object $V$ of $\mathcal V$.

Determine exactly how many natural idempotent endomorphisms of $T$ exist.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the idempotent natural transformations of a tensor-power endofunctor. The main task is to recover the full natural endomorphism algebra from naturality across all vector spaces and linear maps, then analyze its idempotents. Thus Logic, Set Theory, and Foundations -> Category theory is the primary classification.

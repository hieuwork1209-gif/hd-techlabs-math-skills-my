# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be an odd prime and $g\geq2$. Let $\mathcal S_g$ be the one-object groupoid whose automorphism group is
$$
\Gamma_g=\left\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g:\prod_{i=1}^g[a_i,b_i]=1\right\rangle.
$$
Let $\mathcal H_p$ be the one-object groupoid whose automorphism group is the Heisenberg group
$$
H_p=\mathbb F_p^2\times\mathbb F_p
$$
with multiplication
$$
((x,y),z)((x',y'),z')=((x+x',y+y'),z+z'+xy').
$$
Let $\mathcal T\subset\mathcal S_g$ be the subgroupoid generated by $a_1,b_1$.

A functor $F:\mathcal S_g\to\mathcal H_p$ is called globally full if it is full. Consider globally full functors whose restriction
$$
F|_{\mathcal T}:\mathcal T\to\mathcal H_p
$$
is not full.

Let $N_{g,p}$ be the number of natural-isomorphism classes of such functors.

Determine $N_{g,p}$ exactly for every odd prime $p$ and every $g\geq2$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for natural-isomorphism classes of full functors between connected groupoids, subject to a fullness defect on a specified subgroupoid. Passing from functors to the induced automorphism-group homomorphisms and from natural isomorphism to conjugacy is the categorical entry point; the Heisenberg commutator and finite-field counting then resolve the local-to-global compatibility condition. Thus Logic, Set Theory, and Foundations -> Category theory is primary, with finite-group arithmetic serving as the subordinate method.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $0<\theta<1$. Let $(S_x)_{x\in\mathbb Z}$ be a two-sided stationary Markov chain on $\{R,L\}$ with stationary law
$$
\mathbb P(S_x=R)=\theta,
\qquad
\mathbb P(S_x=L)=1-\theta,
$$
and transition matrix
$$
P_\theta=
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{1+4\theta}{5} & \dfrac{4(1-\theta)}5\\[2mm]
\dfrac{4\theta}5 & \dfrac{5-4\theta}{5}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Define
$$
\omega_x=
\begin{cases}
3/4,&S_x=R,\\
1/4,&S_x=L.
\end{cases}
$$
Conditioned on $\omega$, let $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ be the nearest-neighbor random walk on $\mathbb Z$, started at $X_0=0$, with
$$
P_\omega(X_{n+1}=x+1\mid X_n=x)=\omega_x.
$$
Let
$$
T_{\pm1}=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n=\pm1\},
$$
and let $\mathbb P_\theta,\mathbb E_\theta$ denote annealed law and expectation.

There are unique numbers
$$
0<\theta_-<\theta_{\rm dir}<\theta_+<1
$$
such that:
- $X_n\to-\infty$ almost surely for $\theta<\theta_{\rm dir}$, the walk is recurrent at $\theta=\theta_{\rm dir}$, and $X_n\to+\infty$ almost surely for $\theta>\theta_{\rm dir}$;
- $\mathbb E_\theta T_{-1}<\infty$ exactly for $\theta<\theta_-$;
- $\mathbb E_\theta T_1<\infty$ exactly for $\theta>\theta_+$.

Determine $(\theta_-,\theta_{\rm dir},\theta_+)$ exactly.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for three phase-transition parameters of a one-dimensional random walk in a stationary correlated random environment. The direction threshold is controlled by a stationary logarithmic potential, while the two first-passage integrability thresholds depend on correlated products generated by the Markov environment. Thus Probability and Statistics -> Probability foundations is primary.

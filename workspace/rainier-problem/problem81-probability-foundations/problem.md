# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $X_1,\ldots,X_{12}$ be random variables taking values in $\{-1,1\}$. Assume they are unbiased and $8$-wise independent, meaning that every subfamily of at most eight distinct variables is jointly uniform on the corresponding cube $\{-1,1\}^k$.

Determine the largest possible value of
$$
\mathbb P\left(X_1+\cdots+X_{12}=0\right).
$$

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for a sharp extremal probability under finite-wise independence constraints. The central issue is how much an $8$-wise independent distribution on the discrete cube can deviate from full independence while preserving every marginal of size at most eight, together with a matching extremal construction and upper certificate. Thus Probability and Statistics -> Probability foundations is the direct classification.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $K\subset\mathbb R^4$ be the convex hull of the moment curve
$$
(t,t^2,t^3,t^4),\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
$$
Determine exactly the maximum possible $4$-dimensional Euclidean volume of a simplex contained in $K$.

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Topology and Geometry |

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the largest-volume simplex contained in a convex body generated by a moment curve. The central work is an extremal problem for simplices in a convex hull, so Convex geometry is the direct classification.

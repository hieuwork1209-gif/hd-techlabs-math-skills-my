# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $m,n\ge2$ and let $\lambda\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}$. Let $C$ be the smooth projective curve whose function field is
$$
\mathbb C(x)(y,z),
$$
where
$$
y^m=x(x-1),
\qquad
z^n=x(x-\lambda).
$$
Determine the genus $g(C)$ exactly.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the genus of the smooth projective normalization of a fiber product of two cyclic algebraic covers of the projective line. The essential work is to determine the degree and ramification of the resulting algebraic curve and then compute its genus, so Algebraic geometry is the primary classification; covering-space and valuation arguments are used to resolve the normalization and branch structure.

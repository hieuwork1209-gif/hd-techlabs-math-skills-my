# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $r\ge3$, and let $a\in\mathbb Z$ satisfy
$$
a^2\equiv1\pmod r.
$$
Put
$$
h=\gcd(r,2),
\qquad
c=\gcd(r,a-1),
\qquad
d=\gcd\left(c,\frac r h\right),
\qquad
e=\gcd(r,a+1,4).
$$
Fix a primitive $r$-th root of unity $\zeta$. Let $C$ be the smooth projective curve whose function field is
$$
\mathbb C(x)(y,z),
$$
where
$$
y^r=x(x-1),
\qquad
z^r=x(x+1).
$$
The rules
$$
\rho(x,y,z)=(x,\zeta y,\zeta^a z),
\qquad
\tau(x,y,z)=(-x,z,y)
$$
define automorphisms of $C$. Let
$$
H=\langle\rho,\tau\rangle.
$$
Determine exactly the genus of the quotient curve
$$
C/H.
$$

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the genus of a quotient of a smooth algebraic curve by a finite group generated by deck transformations and a symmetry exchanging the two cyclic covers. The decisive mathematics is the ramification and inertia of the algebraic function-field cover together with stabilizers of the finite group action and Riemann-Hurwitz for the quotient. Algebraic geometry is therefore the primary classification.

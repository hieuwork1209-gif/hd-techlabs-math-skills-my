# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
T(\theta)=1+2a\cos\theta+2a_3\cos(3\theta)+2a_4\cos(4\theta)+2a_5\cos(5\theta)+2a_6\cos(6\theta),
$$
where $a,a_3,a_4,a_5,a_6\in\mathbb{R}$. Suppose
$$
T(\theta)\geq0
$$
for every real $\theta$.

Determine the maximum possible value of $a$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for a sharp extremal Fourier coefficient of a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial with a prescribed missing frequency. Its primary content is Analysis and Harmonic analysis: the key structure is spectral factorization of a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial and the interaction of its Fourier coefficients. Finite-dimensional quadratic forms are used only to certify the sharp harmonic-analytic bound.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
T(\theta)=1+2a\cos\theta+2a_4\cos(4\theta)+2a_5\cos(5\theta)+2a_6\cos(6\theta),
$$
where $a,a_4,a_5,a_6\in\mathbb{R}$. Suppose
$$
T(\theta)\geq0
$$
for every real $\theta$.

Determine the maximum possible value of $a$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for a sharp extremal Fourier coefficient of a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial with two prescribed missing frequencies. Its primary content is Analysis and Harmonic analysis: the solution uses a positive quadrature certificate that annihilates the remaining higher Fourier modes, followed by reconstruction of an equality case from its forced contact zeros. Polynomial algebra is subordinate to the harmonic-analytic extremal structure.

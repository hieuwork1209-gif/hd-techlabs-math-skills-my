# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $L$ be the splitting field over $\mathbb Q$ of
$$
(x^4-x-1)(x^3-2).
$$
Determine exactly how many distinct intermediate fields $E$ satisfy
$$
\mathbb Q\subseteq E\subseteq L.
$$

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Abstract Algebra |
| **Sub-domain** | Galois theory |
| **Problem Type** | Exact computation |
| **Answer Type** | Exact scalar |

---

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the complete count of intermediate fields in a finite splitting field over $\mathbb Q$. Determining the relevant Galois group and then using the Galois correspondence to count its subgroups are the central tasks, so Galois theory is the natural classification.

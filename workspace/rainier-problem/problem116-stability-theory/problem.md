# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

For each $A>0$, let $x_A$ be the periodic solution of the Duffing equation
$$
\ddot x+x+x^3=0,
$$
with
$$
x_A(0)=A,
\qquad
\dot x_A(0)=0.
$$
Consider the transverse Hill equation
$$
\ddot y(t)+\bigl(3x_A(t)^2-1\bigr)y(t)=0.
$$
Let $M_A$ be its monodromy matrix over one period of $x_A$.

Determine all amplitudes $A>0$ for which both Floquet multipliers of $M_A$ have modulus $1$.

## Domain Explanation

This is a transverse Floquet-stability problem generated by a nonlinear periodic Duffing orbit. The orbit amplitude changes both the elliptic modulus of the periodic coefficient and the location of the corresponding Hill spectral parameter, so stability is determined by how these quantities move relative to the Floquet bands.

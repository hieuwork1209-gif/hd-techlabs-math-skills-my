# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
0<\alpha<\beta<\frac{\pi}{2},
\qquad
0<\theta<\frac{\pi}{2}.
$$
On the unit sphere $S^2$, use colatitude measured from the north pole and longitude. Let $P$ and $Q$ have colatitude $\beta$ and longitudes $-\theta$ and $\theta$, respectively. Let $C$ be the open polar cap consisting of points with colatitude less than $\alpha$.

Assume that the shorter great-circle arc from $P$ to $Q$ meets $C$. Among all rectifiable curves contained in $S^2\setminus C$ and joining $P$ to $Q$, determine the minimum possible length.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is primarily Topology and Geometry and Differential geometry: it asks for the intrinsic shortest path on a sphere subject to a geodesically meaningful obstacle. The minimizer is governed by the spherical metric, tangency to the cap boundary, and a global length comparison between free geodesic pieces and the constrained boundary segment.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p\ne 5$. Index the vertices of a regular pentagon by $\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z$, and let
$$
D=\langle r,s\mid r^5=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle
$$
be its dihedral symmetry group, acting on the vertices by
$$
r(i)=i+1,
\qquad
s(i)=-i.
$$
Let $\mathbb F_p^5$ have basis $e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4$ indexed by the vertices, with the induced permutation action of $D$, and let
$$
V=\left\{\sum_{i=0}^4 a_i e_i:\sum_{i=0}^4 a_i=0\right\}
$$
be the augmentation submodule.

Let $L$ be the free nilpotent Lie algebra of class $3$ generated by $V$, that is, the free Lie algebra on $V$ modulo all Lie monomials of bracket length at least $4$. The action of $D$ on $V$ extends uniquely to an action by automorphisms of $L$.

Determine
$$
\left|C_{\operatorname{Aut}(L)}(D)\right|
$$
as a closed formula in $p$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the centralizer of a dihedral subgroup inside the automorphism group of a nilpotent Lie algebra. Its main task is group-theoretic: decompose automorphisms into a linear quotient and an IA-kernel, determine which elements commute with the dihedral action, and count the resulting centralizer. Representation-theoretic calculations on the augmentation module and the free Lie layers are supporting tools for this centralizer computation, so Abstract Algebra and Group theory are the most appropriate primary classification.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by
$$
E,F,H,p,q,x,y,t,a,b,c,z.
$$
Assume $z$ is central,
$$
pq-qp=z,
$$
and $x,y,t,a,b,c$ commute with $p,q,z$. Also assume $a,b,c$ commute pairwise and commute with $x,y,t$,
$$
xy-yx=c,\qquad tx-xt=2a,\qquad ty-yt=-2b,
$$
$$
HE-EH=2E,\qquad HF-FH=-2F,\qquad EF-FE=H,
$$
$$
Hp-pH=p,\qquad Hq-qH=-q,
$$
$$
Eq-qE=p,\qquad Fp-pF=q,
$$
$$
Ep=pE,\qquad Fq=qF,
$$
$$
Hx-xH=2x,\qquad Hy-yH=-2y,\qquad Ht=tH,
$$
$$
Ex=xE,\qquad Ey-yE=t,\qquad Et-tE=-2x,
$$
$$
Fx-xF=-t,\qquad Fy=yF,\qquad Ft-tF=2y,
$$
and
$$
Ha-aH=2a,\qquad Hb-bH=-2b,\qquad Hc=cH,
$$
$$
Ea=aE,\qquad Eb-bE=c,\qquad Ec-cE=-2a,
$$
$$
Fa-aF=-c,\qquad Fb=bF,\qquad Fc-cF=2b.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ explicitly as a $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a PBW-type noncommutative algebra, equivalently an enveloping algebra, coupling an $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ action to a Heisenberg pair and two successive current-algebra layers. The next-best classification is Abstract Algebra $\to$ Commutative algebra, since the proof passes to an associated-graded polynomial algebra and computes a Poisson center there. However, those commutative and Poisson calculations are auxiliary: the object whose center is being determined is the original noncommutative algebra, and the decisive steps use PBW localization, lifting central invariants back to the enveloping algebra, and intersecting the localized center with the original ring. Therefore Ring theory is the more appropriate sub-domain.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $x,y,z,w$ subject to
$$
xy=\zeta yx+(1-\zeta)y,
\qquad
xz=\zeta zx+(1-\zeta)z,
$$
$$
yw=\zeta wy+(1-\zeta)y,
\qquad
zw=\zeta wz+(1-\zeta)z,
$$
$$
yz=zy,
\qquad
xw-wx=(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})yz.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves the center and commutation structure of a noncommutative algebra at a root of unity, which are core topics in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The solution also uses an affine change of generators, a PBW normal form, and a hidden determinant-like central element to control the algebra. However, those structures serve the ring-theoretic center calculation rather than making the problem primarily a linear algebra or commutative algebra task.

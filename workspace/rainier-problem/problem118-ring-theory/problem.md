# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $u,v,w,s,X,Y$ such that $u,v,w,s$ commute pairwise, $XY=YX$, and
$$
Xu=uX,
\qquad Xv=vX+u,
\qquad Xw=wX,
$$
$$
Xs=sX+(1-u)w+2uv+u^3,
$$
$$
Yu=uY,
\qquad Yv=vY+u^2,
\qquad Yw=wY+u,
$$
and
$$
Ys=sY+u(1-u)w+(1-u+2u^2)v+u^2+u^4.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra presented by coupled differential-type skew relations, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The presentation hides both a polynomial change of coefficient variables and a nonconstant change of skew generators. In the correct coordinates the problem becomes a joint-kernel calculation for two commuting locally nilpotent derivations, followed by a PBW argument excluding positive skew degree.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by
$$
E,F,H,p,q,z.
$$
Assume $z$ is central,
$$
pq-qp=z,
$$
$$
HE-EH=2E,\qquad HF-FH=-2F,\qquad EF-FE=H,
$$
and
$$
Hp-pH=p,\qquad Hq-qH=-q,
$$
$$
Eq-qE=p,\qquad Fp-pF=q,
$$
$$
Ep=pE,\qquad Fq=qF.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ explicitly as a $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a PBW-type noncommutative algebra combining an $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ action with a Heisenberg relation. The central Heisenberg parameter permits a localization in which a hidden commuting $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ copy appears only after a nontrivial oscillator shift, and the resulting localized center must then be intersected back with the original algebra.

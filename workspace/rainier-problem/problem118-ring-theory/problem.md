# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $h,s,t,x,y$ subject to
$$
hs=sh,\qquad ht=th,\qquad st=ts,
$$
$$
xh=\zeta hx,
\qquad
xs=\zeta^2(s+h^2)x,
\qquad
xt=\zeta^3(t+hs+2h^3)x,
$$
$$
yh=\zeta^{-1}hy,
\qquad
ys=\zeta^{-2}(s-h^2)y,
\qquad
yt=\zeta^{-3}(t-hs-h^3)y,
$$
and
$$
yx=xy.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by coupled skew relations, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The relations hide an infinite-order triangular automorphism whose fixed ring is controlled by a locally nilpotent derivation and a nonlinear polynomial invariant. The center calculation then combines that invariant theory with the grading induced by the two skew generators.

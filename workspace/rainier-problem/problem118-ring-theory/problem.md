# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$, and let $A=k[u,v,w]$. Put
$$
\Delta=v^2+uw,
$$
$$
P=2(u-2v\Delta-w\Delta^2)-(v+w\Delta)+2w,
$$
$$
Q=3(v+w\Delta)-2w,
\qquad
E=Q^2+5wP.
$$
Let $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_k(A)$ be defined by
$$
\sigma(u)=P+2QE-5wE^2,
$$
$$
\sigma(v)=Q-5wE,
\qquad
\sigma(w)=5w.
$$
Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $A,x,y$ subject to
$$
xf=\sigma(f)x,
\qquad
yf=\sigma^{-1}(f)y
\qquad(f\in A),
$$
and
$$
yx=xy.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a skew polynomial algebra defined by a nonlinear polynomial automorphism, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The automorphism is a nonlinear conjugate of a simple expanding action, but the conjugating coordinates are hidden inside a Nagata-type transformation. The center calculation requires uncovering that structure, determining the fixed ring, and then controlling the skew grading.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $h,x,y$ subject to
$$
xh=\zeta hx,
\qquad
yh=\zeta^{-1}hy,
$$
and
$$
yx-xy=(\zeta^{-1}-1)h+(\zeta^{-2}-1)h^2.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by root-of-unity skew relations, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The inhomogeneous commutator hides a central Casimir-type element that must be recovered from the interaction of the three generators. The root-of-unity symmetry then controls which powers can occur in the full center.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $a,b,c,d,X,Y,Z$. Assume $a,b,c,d$ commute pairwise and
$$
Xa=aX+3b,
\qquad Xb=bX+a+2c,
$$
$$
Xc=cX+2b+d,
\qquad Xd=dX+3c,
$$
$$
Ya=aY-3b,
\qquad Yb=bY+a-2c,
$$
$$
Yc=cY+2b-d,
\qquad Yd=dY+3c,
$$
$$
Za=aZ+3a,
\qquad Zb=bZ+b,
$$
$$
Zc=cZ-c,
\qquad Zd=dZ-3d,
$$
and
$$
ZX-XZ=2Y,
\qquad ZY-YZ=2X,
\qquad XY-YX=-2Z.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by coupled linear commutation relations. The main task is to determine all polynomial combinations that commute with every generator and to prove that no additional terms involving the noncommuting generators can occur.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$, and let $m\geq2$ be an integer. Let $R_m$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $x,y,z$ subject to
$$
x^{5m+1}=0,
$$
$$
\bigl(1+(1-\zeta)x\bigr)yx=\zeta xy+(1-\zeta)x^2,
$$
$$
\bigl(1+(1-\zeta^{-1})x\bigr)zx=\zeta^{-1}xz+(1-\zeta^{-1})x^2,
$$
and
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1+x)^2zy={}&\zeta(1+x)^2yz+(\zeta^{-1}-\zeta)x(1+x)z\\
&+(1-\zeta^2)x(1+x)y+(\zeta^2-\zeta^{-1})x^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Let $Z(R_m)$ denote its center. Determine $Z(R_m)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R_m$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves the center of a noncommutative algebra with coupled twisted commutation laws, which are core topics in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The solution also uses finite-order skew automorphisms, a nilpotent coordinate change, and a congruence lattice controlling central monomials. However, those structures serve the ring-theoretic center calculation rather than making the problem primarily a linear algebra or commutative algebra task.

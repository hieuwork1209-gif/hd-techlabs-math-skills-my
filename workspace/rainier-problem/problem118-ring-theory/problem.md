# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $p,q,c,X,Y,Z$. Assume $c$ is central and
$$
pq-qp=c,
$$
$$
Xp=pX+q,
\qquad
Xq=qX+p,
$$
$$
Yp=pY-q,
\qquad
Yq=qY+p,
$$
$$
Zp=pZ+p,
\qquad
Zq=qZ-q,
$$
and
$$
ZX-XZ=2Y,
\qquad
ZY-YZ=2X,
\qquad
XY-YX=-2Z.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by coupled Heisenberg-type and three-generator commutation relations. Determining the center requires controlling both the noncommuting coefficient pair and the additional skew generators, together with possible mixed central combinations.

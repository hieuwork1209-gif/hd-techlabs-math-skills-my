# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by
$$
E,F,H,a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4.
$$
Assume $a_0,\ldots,a_4$ commute pairwise,
$$
HE-EH=2E,\qquad HF-FH=-2F,\qquad EF-FE=H,
$$
and, for $0\le i\le4$,
$$
Ha_i-a_iH=(4-2i)a_i,
$$
$$
Ea_i-a_iE=i\,a_{i-1},\qquad Fa_i-a_iF=(4-i)a_{i+1},
$$
where $a_{-1}=a_5=0$.

Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ explicitly as a $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a PBW-type noncommutative algebra arising from a semidirect-product Lie algebra. Determining the center requires controlling the Poisson center of the associated graded algebra, proving that the abelian radical removes dependence on the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ variables, and then identifying the full invariant ring of the resulting five-dimensional module.

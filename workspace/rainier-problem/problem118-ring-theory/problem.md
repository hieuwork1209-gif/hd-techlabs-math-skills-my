# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $a,b,c,u,v,w,X,Y,Z$. Assume $a,b,c,u,v,w$ commute pairwise, $X,Y,Z$ commute pairwise and commute with $a,b,c$, and
$$
Xu=uX-a(b^2+c),
$$
$$
Xv=vX+a^2b+2a(b^2+c)u-c,
$$
$$
Xw=wX+a^2+b+cu-a^2bu+a(b^2+c)v-2a(b^2+c)u^2,
$$
$$
Yu=uY+b^2+c,
$$
$$
Yv=vY-ab-2(b^2+c)u,
$$
$$
Yw=wY-a+abu-(b^2+c)v+2(b^2+c)u^2,
$$
$$
Zu=uZ-b,
\qquad
Zv=vZ+a+2bu,
$$
and
$$
Zw=wZ+bv-au-2bu^2.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a differential Ore-type algebra with three coupled skew generators. The commutator derivations have a hidden polynomial syzygy, so the center contains both a coefficient invariant and a nontrivial positive-skew-degree element. Determining the full center requires finding both joint kernels and proving that no other skew polynomial survives.

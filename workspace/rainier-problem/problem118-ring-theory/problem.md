# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $h,s,x,y$ subject to
$$
hs=sh,
$$
$$
xh=\zeta hx,
\qquad
xs=\zeta^2sx,
$$
$$
yh=\zeta^{-1}hy,
\qquad
ys=\zeta^{-2}sy,
$$
and
$$
yx-xy=(\zeta^{-1}-1)h+(\zeta^{-2}-1)s+(\zeta^{-3}-1)hs+hs^2.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by coupled root-of-unity skew relations, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. A resonant term in the inhomogeneous commutator prevents the expected fifth-power periodicity and forces a generalized Weyl algebra analysis with a nontrivial translation. The final center is then determined by combining that obstruction with the invariant subring of a weighted fifth-root action.

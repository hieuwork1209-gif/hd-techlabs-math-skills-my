# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. For integers $n,r$ with $n\geq2$ and $2\leq r\leq n$, let $R_{n,r}$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $x,y$ subject to
$$
x^{n+1}=0,
\qquad
yx=xy+x^r.
$$
Let $Z(R_{n,r})$ denote its center. Determine $Z(R_{n,r})$ as an explicit subalgebra of $R_{n,r}$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves the center and commutator structure of a noncommutative algebra, which are core topics in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The solution also uses a derivation and a normal-form decomposition to analyze which coefficients can commute with the generators. However, those tools serve the ring-theoretic center calculation rather than turning the problem into a commutative algebra or linear algebra task.

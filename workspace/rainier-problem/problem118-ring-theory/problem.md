# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $a,b,c,u,X,Y,Z$. Put
$$
q=b+a^2,
\qquad
r=c+ab.
$$
Assume $a,b,c,u$ commute pairwise, $X,Y,Z$ commute pairwise and commute with $a,b,c$, and
$$
Xu=uX+a(1-q+qr),
$$
$$
Yu=uY+q(1-r),
$$
and
$$
Zu=uZ+r.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a differential Ore-type algebra with three commuting skew generators. After a hidden triangular change of skew variables, centrality is controlled by the kernel of a locally nilpotent derivation whose syzygy module is not free. The full center requires computing that invariant ring together with its saturation over the polynomial coefficient ring.

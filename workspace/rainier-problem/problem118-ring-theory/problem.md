# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$, let $A=k[u,v,w]$, and put
$$
\Delta=v^2+uw,
\quad p=u-2v\Delta-w\Delta^2,
\quad q=v+w\Delta,
\quad r=w.
$$
For
$$
(P,Q,R)=(-8p+19q-98r,-27q+98r,-125r)
$$
and
$$
(\widetilde P,\widetilde Q,\widetilde R)=\left(-\frac14p-\frac5{36}q+\frac{16}{225}r,-\frac19q-\frac{16}{225}r,-\frac1{25}r\right),
$$
set
$$
E=Q^2+PR,
\qquad
\widetilde E=\widetilde Q^2+\widetilde P\widetilde R.
$$
Define $\sigma,\tau\in\operatorname{Aut}_k(A)$ by
$$
\sigma(u)=P+2QE-RE^2,
\quad \sigma(v)=Q-RE,
\quad \sigma(w)=R,
$$
$$
\tau(u)=\widetilde P+2\widetilde Q\widetilde E-\widetilde R\widetilde E^2,
\quad \tau(v)=\widetilde Q-\widetilde R\widetilde E,
\quad \tau(w)=\widetilde R.
$$
Let $R$ be generated by $A,x,y,z,t$ subject to
$$
xf=\sigma(f)x,
\quad yf=\sigma^{-1}(f)y,
\quad zf=\tau(f)z,
\quad tf=\tau^{-1}(f)t
\qquad(f\in A),
$$
and assume $x,y,z,t$ commute pairwise. Determine $Z(R)$ explicitly.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of an iterated skew polynomial algebra controlled by two commuting polynomial automorphisms. The center depends on their common invariant ring and on the integer relation lattice between the two actions, which are ring-theoretic questions in noncommutative algebra.

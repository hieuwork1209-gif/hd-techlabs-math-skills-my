# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by
$$
E,F,H,p,q,x,y,t,z.
$$
Assume $z$ is central,
$$
pq-qp=z,
$$
and $x,y,t$ commute pairwise and commute with $p,q,z$. Also assume
$$
HE-EH=2E,\qquad HF-FH=-2F,\qquad EF-FE=H,
$$
$$
Hp-pH=p,\qquad Hq-qH=-q,
$$
$$
Eq-qE=p,\qquad Fp-pF=q,
$$
$$
Ep=pE,\qquad Fq=qF,
$$
and
$$
Hx-xH=2x,\qquad Hy-yH=-2y,\qquad Ht=tH,
$$
$$
Ex=xE,\qquad Ey-yE=t,\qquad Et-tE=-2x,
$$
$$
Fx-xF=-t,\qquad Fy=yF,\qquad Ft-tF=2y.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ explicitly as a $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a PBW-type noncommutative algebra coupling an $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ action simultaneously to a Heisenberg pair and to an abelian adjoint module. Solving it requires first removing the Heisenberg action by localization and an oscillator shift, then computing the center of the residual Takiff algebra, and finally intersecting the localized center back with the original algebra.

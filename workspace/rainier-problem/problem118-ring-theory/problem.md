# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $p,q,r,U,V,W$. Assume $p,q,r$ commute pairwise and
$$
Wp=pW+2q,
\qquad Wq=qW+2p,
\qquad Wr=rW,
$$
$$
Up=pU,
\qquad Uq=qU-2r,
\qquad Ur=rU-2q,
$$
$$
Vp=pV+2r,
\qquad Vq=qV,
\qquad Vr=rV-2p,
$$
and
$$
WU-UW=2V+2q,
$$
$$
WV-VW=2U+2p,
$$
$$
UV-VU=-2W-2r.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a PBW-type noncommutative algebra defined by coupled linear commutation relations. The relations hide a nontrivial semidirect-product structure, and the center depends on identifying the correct invariant quadratic combinations and proving that they generate all central elements.

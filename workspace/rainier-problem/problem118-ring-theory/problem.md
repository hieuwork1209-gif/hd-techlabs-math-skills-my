# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$. Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $a,b,c,d,x,y$ subject to
$$
ab=ba,\quad ac=ca,\quad ad=da,\quad bc=cb,\quad bd=db,\quad cd=dc,
$$
$$
xa=-ax,
\qquad
xb=-(b+a)x,
$$
$$
xc=-(c+2b+a)x,
\qquad
xd=-(d+3c+3b+a)x,
$$
$$
ya=-ay,
\qquad
yb=(a-b)y,
$$
$$
yc=(-c+2b-a)y,
\qquad
yd=(-d+3c-3b+a)y,
$$
and
$$
yx=xy.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by coupled skew relations, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The skew action hides an infinite-order unipotent component whose polynomial fixed ring has nontrivial algebraic structure. The center calculation requires identifying that fixed ring and then controlling the grading contributed by the two skew generators.

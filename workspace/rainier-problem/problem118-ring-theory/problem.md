# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive cube root of unity $\omega$, and let $m\geq2$ be an integer. Let $R_m$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $x,y$ subject to
$$
x^{3m+1}=0,
\qquad
\bigl(1+(1-\omega)x\bigr)yx=\omega xy+(1-\omega)x^2.
$$
Let $Z(R_m)$ denote its center. Determine $Z(R_m)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R_m$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves the center of a noncommutative algebra defined by a nilpotent relation and a twisted commutation law, which are core topics in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The solution also uses a finite-order automorphism, an inner skew derivation, and a change of nilpotent coordinate to expose the hidden structure of the relation. However, those ingredients serve the ring-theoretic center calculation rather than making the problem primarily a linear algebra or commutative algebra task.

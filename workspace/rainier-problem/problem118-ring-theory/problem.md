# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$, and let $A=k[u,v,w]$. Set
$$
U=u+1-3(v+u^2)^2-8\bigl(w+u(v+u^2)\bigr)^2.
$$
Let $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_k(A)$ be defined by
$$
\sigma(u)=U,
$$
$$
\sigma(v)=2(v+u^2)-U^2,
$$
and
$$
\sigma(w)=3\bigl(w+u(v+u^2)\bigr)-2U(v+u^2).
$$
Let $R$ be the unital $k$-algebra generated by $A,x,y$ subject to
$$
xf=\sigma(f)x,
\qquad
yf=\sigma^{-1}(f)y
\qquad(f\in A),
$$
and
$$
yx=xy.
$$
Let $Z(R)$ denote the center of $R$. Determine $Z(R)$ as an explicit $k$-subalgebra of $R$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the center of a skew polynomial algebra defined by a nonlinear polynomial automorphism, which is a core topic in Abstract Algebra and Ring theory. The main difficulty is to uncover coordinates that reveal the dynamics of the automorphism and determine its polynomial fixed ring. The center then follows by combining that fixed-ring calculation with the grading of the two skew generators.

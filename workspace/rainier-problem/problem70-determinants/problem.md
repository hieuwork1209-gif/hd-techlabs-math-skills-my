# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $L$ be the splitting field over $\mathbb Q$ of $x^{16}-2$. How many intermediate fields $E$ with
$$
\mathbb Q\subseteq E\subseteq L
$$
satisfy $[E:\mathbb Q]=8$?

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Abstract Algebra |
| **Sub-domain** | Field theory |
| **Problem Type** | Exact computation |
| **Answer Type** | Exact scalar |

---

## Domain Explanation

The central objects are a splitting field and its intermediate fields. The degree condition is converted by the Galois correspondence into a subgroup-counting problem only after the field extension and its automorphisms are determined, so Abstract Algebra -> Field theory is the fundamental classification rather than Linear Algebra or Number Theory.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $m\ge2$. Determine the number of ordered pairs
$$
(A,B)\in M_2(\mathbb Z/2^m\mathbb Z)^2
$$
satisfying
$$
A^2=B^2=AB=BA=0.
$$

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Number Theory |
| **Sub-domain** | Modular arithmetic and congruences |
| **Problem Type** | Exact computation |
| **Answer Type** | Integer |

---

## Domain Explanation

The problem counts rank-two representations over the finite local ring $\mathbb Z/2^m\mathbb Z$ of the radical-square-zero algebra with two generators. The four relations force the two endomorphisms to have compatible image and kernel filtrations. Reduction modulo $2$ produces a zero branch and nine primitive branches, while lifting the zero branch gives a two-step two-adic recurrence. The central structure is therefore modular and two-adic, so the best classification is Number Theory with sub-domain Modular arithmetic and congruences.

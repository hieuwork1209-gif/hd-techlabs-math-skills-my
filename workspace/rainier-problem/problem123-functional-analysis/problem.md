# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
H=\left\{f\in L^2(0,1):\int_0^1 x f(x)\,dx=0\right\},
$$
and let $T:H\to L^2(0,1)$ be the restriction of the Volterra operator
$$
(Tf)(x)=\int_0^x f(t)\,dt.
$$
Determine the exact operator norm
$$
\|T\|_{H\to L^2(0,1)}.
$$

## Domain Explanation

The requested object is the operator norm of a bounded linear integral operator restricted to a closed subspace of the Hilbert space $L^2(0,1)$, so the primary mathematics is functional analysis. The variational and boundary-value calculations used to evaluate the norm are auxiliary tools rather than the object being classified or computed.

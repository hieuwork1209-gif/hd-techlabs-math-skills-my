# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $H_0$ be the closed subspace of $L^2(0,1)$ consisting of functions with integral zero. For $f\in H_0$, define
$$
(Tf)(x)=\int_0^x (x-t)f(t)\,dt.
$$
Determine exactly the operator norm of $T:H_0\to L^2(0,1)$.

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |

| **Domain** | Analysis |

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the exact norm of a compact integral operator on a closed subspace of a Hilbert space. The decisive structure is the spectrum of the associated positive compact operator, so Functional analysis is the natural classification.

# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix a real parameter $a$ with $0<a<\sqrt{2}$. For real $t$, define
$$
A(t)=e^{it}+e^{i\sqrt{2}t}+e^{i\sqrt{3}t}+e^{-i(1+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3})t}
$$
and
$$
B(t)=e^{2it}+e^{2i\sqrt{2}t}+e^{2i\sqrt{3}t}+e^{-2i(1+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3})t}.
$$
Determine, in terms of $a$, the set of all $L\in\mathbb{C}$ for which there is a sequence of real numbers $t_n\to\infty$ satisfying
$$
A(t_n)\to a(1+i)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
B(t_n)\to L.
$$

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves conditional cluster sets of complex-valued oscillatory functions and subsequences at infinity, which are part of Calculus and its Limits and continuity sub-domain. The problem also involves dense torus orbits and self-inversive polynomial root constraints, which are part of dynamical systems and algebra. However, those tools only describe the admissible subsequential limits, while the requested object is the complete limit region.

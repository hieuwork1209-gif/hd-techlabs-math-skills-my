# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix $a>0$. Let $\Theta\sim\operatorname{Beta}(a,a)$, so
$$
P(\Theta\in d\theta)=\frac{\theta^{a-1}(1-\theta)^{a-1}}{B(a,a)}\,d\theta,
\qquad 0<\theta<1.
$$
Conditional on $\Theta=\theta$, let $(X_i)_{i\ge1}$ and $(Y_i)_{i\ge1}$ be independent sequences, each consisting of iid Bernoulli random variables with
$$
P(X_i=1\mid\Theta=\theta)
=P(Y_i=1\mid\Theta=\theta)
=4\theta(1-\theta).
$$
Using natural logarithms, determine the exact function of $a$
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\left[
I\bigl((X_1,\ldots,X_n);(Y_1,\ldots,Y_n)\bigr)
-\frac12\log n
\right].
$$
You may use
$$
B(x,y)=\frac{\Gamma(x)\Gamma(y)}{\Gamma(x+y)},
\qquad
\psi(x)=\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)}.
$$

## Domain Explanation

The requested object is the asymptotic mutual information between two dependent random blocks generated by a common latent variable, so the primary mathematics is joint distributions and dependence. Beta-posterior calculations and entropy asymptotics are auxiliary tools used to evaluate that dependence functional rather than the quantity being classified.

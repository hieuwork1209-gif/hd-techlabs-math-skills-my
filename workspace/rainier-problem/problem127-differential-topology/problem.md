# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $m\ge2$ and $t>0$. Regard
$$
S^{2m-1}=\{(z_1,\dots,z_m)\in\mathbb C^m:\ |z_1|^2+\cdots+|z_m|^2=1\}
$$
with its standard orientation. Using cyclic indices $z_{m+1}=z_1$, define
$$
P_t(z_1,\dots,z_m)
=\bigl(z_1^2+t\overline{z_2},\ z_2^2+t\overline{z_3},\dots,\ z_m^2+t\overline{z_1}\bigr).
$$
Assume that $P_t(z)\ne0$ for every $z\in S^{2m-1}$, and define
$$
F_t(z)=\frac{P_t(z)}{\|P_t(z)\|}.
$$
Determine the Brouwer degree of
$$
F_t:S^{2m-1}\to S^{2m-1}
$$
in terms of $m$ and $t$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is primarily Topology and Geometry and Differential topology: it asks for the Brouwer degree of a smooth parameterized family of self-maps of an odd-dimensional sphere. Determining the degree requires understanding when the normalization is valid and how the orientation class behaves across the admissible parameter regimes.

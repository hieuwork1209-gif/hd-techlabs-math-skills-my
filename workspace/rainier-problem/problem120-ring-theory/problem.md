# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p\equiv1\pmod 4$ be a prime and let
$$
R=M_4(\mathbb F_p).
$$
Determine the number of ordered triples $(E_1,E_2,E_3)$ of subrings of $R$, each containing the identity matrix and each a field of order $p^2$, such that for every distinct $i,j\in\{1,2,3\}$ and every $A\in E_i$, $B\in E_j$ satisfying
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)=\operatorname{tr}(B)=0,
$$
one has
$$
AB=-BA.
$$

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for embedded quadratic fields inside a finite matrix ring subject to pairwise anticommutation. The decisive structure is the algebra generated by normalized trace-zero generators, which is a Clifford algebra acting on a four-dimensional module. Ring theory is therefore primary; linear algebra enters only through the representation and orbit count.

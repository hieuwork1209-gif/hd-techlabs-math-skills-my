# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
V=\mathbb{F}_2^6.
$$
Let $\mathcal F$ be a family of $3$-dimensional subspaces of $V$ such that
$$
U\cap W\neq\{0\}
$$
for every two distinct $U,W\in\mathcal F$.

Determine the maximum possible value of
$$
|\mathcal F|.
$$

---

## Domain Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Discrete Mathematics and Combinatorics |
| **Sub-domain** | Extremal combinatorics |
| **Problem Type** | Optimization |
| **Answer Type** | Exact scalar |

---

## Domain Explanation

This problem asks for the largest pairwise-intersecting family of fixed-dimensional subspaces of a finite vector space. The decisive argument is extremal: a spread gives a universal averaging bound, and a point-star construction attains it. Therefore Discrete Mathematics and Combinatorics / Extremal combinatorics is the primary classification. Design theory and finite geometry is secondary because spreads and finite projective geometry provide the certificate and construction.

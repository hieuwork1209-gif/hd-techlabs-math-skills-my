# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p>3$ be a prime, and let $\left(\frac{\cdot}{p}\right)$ denote the Legendre symbol. Evaluate
$$
P_p=\prod_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\left(\frac{k^2+k+1}{p}\right)
$$
in terms of $p$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is primarily Number Theory and Quadratic residues and reciprocity: it asks for an exact half-range product of Legendre symbols, and the decisive arithmetic distinction is whether $-3$ is a quadratic residue modulo $p$. Quadratic reciprocity identifies that residue class, while a finite-field product factorization resolves the nonvanishing case; the extension-field calculation is subordinate to the quadratic-residue structure.
